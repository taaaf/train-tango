How to play

how to play

The board

Every phone becomes one tile of a shared track. A single loop of rail winds around the outside of all the phones, and trains run around it continuously, crossing from one screen to the next.

Trains

Each train is a locomotive (triangle) plus a few cars (circles). Every car holds one passenger, and trains never turn around — they just keep looping.

A train carries only one color at a time. Once someone boards, only that color can join until the train empties out. The locomotive tints to the color it's carrying.

Stations

Stations are colored platforms beside the track. The gate is closed by default, so trains stop to drop off matching passengers and pick up the queue.

When boarding ends the train waits — tap the gate to open it and send it off. The gate auto-closes once the last car clears. Open it before a train arrives to let it blow past.

Wait for the train to board, then tap the gate to release it.

Passengers

Passengers wait in a station's queue, each with a color. A train drops them only at a station of that same color. Each delivery scores points.

Junctions

A junction is a tunnel shortcut. Its two mouths sit roughly opposite on the loop, so diverting a train cuts its trip about in half.

Tap the lever to divert; tap again for straight. Handy for rushing a train to the far side of the board.

Tap the lever to toggle the diversion.

Boost pads

The run of white arrows is a boost pad. Swipe along it, the way the arrows point, to arm it: the arrows light one by one under your finger and the whole run turns bright blue once it takes. The next train over gets a 3× burst for 6 seconds, then the pad recharges. Swipe back the other way to disarm it.

Pads are swiped rather than tapped so they can sit right next to a station gate without the two stealing each other’s taps.

Swipe along the arrows to arm the pad.

Powerups

Each powerup exists once in the whole game and is dealt to one phone, so you each hold different ones — say out loud what you have. Tap to buy with points; the ring around the button fills as you can afford it.

The ring is cut into one band per level. Points fill it band by band, and the moment the first band is full the powerup is ready to tap. Fill further bands and you can buy further levels — a ring filled to the top means you can max it out right now.

Every level you buy turns a band orange and adds 30 seconds. The orange then burns back down one band at a time, and the bands still lit are your level — buy all three and you run at level 3 for 30 seconds, level 2 for the next 30, level 1 for the last 30.

So topping up is never wasted: a level bought late just stacks another 30 seconds on the end. Buy early and you spend longer at the top level, which is the real reason to commit.

The five: faster boarding, more cars per train, freeze, one extra train, and reverse direction.

Freeze — and pointing it

Freeze and the extra train need a second tap to pick a target, and that tap can come from any phone. Buy one and the game stops: every screen dims down to just the things you can tap, and nothing moves until somebody picks. Take your time — the clock doesn't start running until play resumes. Each freeze level lets you ice one more station.

For a freeze, tap a station. For the extra train, tap anywhere on the track — it snaps to the nearest point, and slides clear if a train is already sitting there.

A frozen station ices over: it takes no new passengers, and its overflow and double-points timers pause. Use it to rescue a station near overflow.

Tap the snowflake to arm it, then tap the station to freeze it.

Reverse direction

Flips every train to run the other way around the loop — clockwise to counter-clockwise. It happens instantly and stays until someone buys it again, so it is the one powerup with no timer. Stations get served in the opposite order, which can rescue a queue you were never going to reach in time.

Double-points window

Every few minutes one color grows a dashed ring. It draws closed (arming), then marches — while marching, deliveries to that color pay 2×. Prioritize it!

Full train — double points

Fill every car and the whole train tints to its color — it's full. Every delivery then pays 2×, so top a train up before unloading. It stays latched until the train empties.

This stacks with the double-points window — a full train delivered to a bonus color scores 4×.

It gets harder

Trains get faster on their own as the game goes on — that is not something you buy any more, and it tops out after about eight minutes. Passengers also arrive quicker and the track starts to wobble. Coordinate, spend your powerups, and don't let any station overflow.

Overflow — game over!

If a station's queue fills up and stays full too long, the game ends. A full station blinks as a warning — open its gate and route a train there fast.
